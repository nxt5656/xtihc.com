Author 知乎用户 pZgyIDE, edited November 2, 2026, at 09:52:

> Back in '22, I was working in Shanghai and nearly starved to death. The announcement said April 1–April 5, just four days, but the lockdown lasted until June 1. I'd only stocked up on five days of food, and I didn't receive the community's first food distribution until April 18.
>
> I couldn't get any groceries. When I called the police, they told me to contact the community office, but nobody answered the phone there. I was ready to force my way through a checkpoint just to get prison food. When you're desperate to survive, nothing else matters.
>
> By the end of the year, everything reopened and it was time for herd immunity. Sometimes, when you're speechless, you really do laugh.
>
> Ever since then, **sacrificing individual interests for a collective victory has left me cold. That victory may well not belong to me, but I could be the one who pays the price.**
